Who We Are

Driving Digital Empowerment & Entrepreneurship for a Self-Reliant India

The Foundation for Empowering Digital Marketing and Entrepreneurship (FEDME) is a legally registered Public Charitable Trust (Reg. No. 339) committed to creating sustainable, scalable, and meaningful impact across society.

Our Purpose & Dedication

We believe that true national progress begins at the grassroots level when every individual has access to modern healthcare, proper nutrition, and quality education. Our foundation bridges critical socio-economic gaps by bringing essential infrastructure and digital solutions directly to underserved communities.

By collaborating with government organizations, Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) partners, and local leaders, we drive high-impact programs ranging from mobile dialysis units and Poshan nutrition kits to smart learning environments. We aim to nurture self-reliant individuals, foster youth entrepreneurship, and build healthier, educated generations.

To conduct and manage Poshan Programs for nutrition security and child health for the general public at large.

Educational & Healthcare Products

To provide and arrange essential Educational Products and Healthcare Products for underserved communities.

Resource Supply Drives

Digital & IT Services

To help and provide Digital and IT Services, computer education, and technical assistance for needy people.

Digital Skill Literacy

Scholarships & Financial Aid

To give financial assistance by way of studentships, scholarships, stipends, books, uniforms, or meals for needy students.

Educational Welfare

Medical Help & Medicines

To arrange and provide all types of medical help, medicine & treatment facilities especially for poor and needy people.

Lifesaving Healthcare Aid
